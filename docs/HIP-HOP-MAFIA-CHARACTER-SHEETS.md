## The Made Deck Hierarchy Overview

The underworld of Planet Ongaku counts itself in cards. Fifty-four cards divided into four houses plus two Jokers, running on the same ladder as the Sick Deck ($10$ weakest $\to$ $\text{Ace}$ strongest):

- **♠ Spades (The Founders' House):** Culture, masters, publishing, and the central Commission throne.
- **♥ Hearts (The Velvet House):** Nightlife, clubs, VIP hospitality, and clean glamour cash.
- **♣ Clubs (The Corner House):** Ground-level corners, enforcers, and territory protection.
- **♦ Diamonds (The Count House):** Money laundering, banks, exotic cars, and stolen technology.
- **🃏 Jokers (The Commission Advisors):** Special functions answering directly to the Chairman (Books & Intelligence).
- **◎ Signed Tier:** Unsigned and signed musical talent trapped under contractual leverage.
