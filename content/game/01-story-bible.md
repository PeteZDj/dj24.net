# DJ24 XD — Story Bible

## Logline
A broke, gifted kid wins a two-year scholarship to the **Royal Music School** the same week a
**politician guns down his father on live TV** and walks free. He claws his way up through four
regions of Planet Ongaku — and by the end he *is* the monster he set out to punish.

## The "Z Story" (PeteZDj)
The protagonist's journey is a descent told in three names:

> **Kid → General Grievous → Z**

- **Kid** — a 22-year-old (post-university / post-high-school) who just wants justice and to keep
  his family afloat.
- **General Grievous** — a feared warlord of sound who collects crews, venues and enemies.
- **Z** — the crowned ruler of Ongaku Prime; the antagonist the *next* heroes of the saga will have
  to stop. The finale, **Regicide**, is a horror-tinged point of no return.

This is deliberately a **shōnen-style protagonist**: short-tempered yet cool, serious yet clumsy,
a good-for-nothing with hidden ability — but his arc bends from hero to villain.

## Three threads, one spine
1. **The Family Thread** — the murder on live TV, the failed court case, "no justice," and the
   slow corruption of a good kid who keeps being told the system won't save him.
2. **The Come-Up Thread** — house parties → residencies → a scholarship → an industry empire →
   the capital. This is the gameplay spine (see the episode guide).
3. **The Meta-War Thread** — the larger DJ24 universe conflict that Pete stumbles into and
   ultimately exploits.

## The Meta-War: Musicians vs. Komedians
- The story opens in the middle of a **civil war between music genres** on Planet Ongaku
  (cold-open energy of the "snakes" episode of *Rick and Morty* — chaos that escalates fast).
- Suddenly the **Komedians** arrive and begin **mining the planet by drilling**. Nobody knows who
  they are. They look *other* — dark, hairy, monkey-like — but they are roughly **1178% smarter**
  than the musicians.
- Musicians confront them; the Komedians jokingly attack the musicians' top brass.
- Outmatched, the **Musicians must gang up** across genres to stand a chance.
- They set up a **command HQ** to gather intel (think CIA / mission control). They start knowing
  **nothing** and have to dig — slowly learning how the government sectors and **grandmasters** of
  Ongaku really operate and who is in command.
- Their only real lead is a single **abandoned Komedian spaceship**.
- **Theme:** the story is about *current human limitations* — and what it costs to exceed them.

Pete's villain turn is the dark answer to this theme: he beats the limits by **giving up his
humanity**, taking Komedian power and becoming something post-human.

## Tone & references
- City/planet feel: **"Hammer the city"** — a living, neon megacity in the spirit of
  *Cyberpunk 2077*'s Night City.
- Gameplay structure & mission density: **GTA: San Andreas** (mission givers, objectives,
  scaling rewards, area-gated progression).
- Switching characters: **Watch Dogs: Legion**.
- DJ battles: **Naruto**-style technique reads/counters in a battle circle.
- School chaos: **Bully**.

## From 437 → 101 episodes
The original dream was a **437-episode** saga. DJ24 XD compresses the *origin* of the villain into a
tight **101**, leaving room to continue. Original arc lengths (kept for reference / future seasons):

| Arc | Episodes |
| --- | ---: |
| Billionaire X | 76 |
| Soundman | 31 |
| Factor C | 39 |
| Dj Strange | 56 |
| Everyone's a DJ | 114 |
| Dj Grievous | 88 |
| Dj Regicide | 13 |
| **DJ24 total** | **437** |

The 101-episode game folds **Billionaire X**, **Dj Grievous**, and **Dj Regicide** into its second
half, and reserves the others as continuation seasons (New Game+).
