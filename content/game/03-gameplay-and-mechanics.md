# DJ24 XD — Gameplay & Mechanics

An open-world **music RPG**: GTA-San-Andreas mission structure on a city you can also **build**.
Fighting, guns, drugs, ambulances, SWAT, and street racing all coexist with decks and dancefloors.

## Core loop
**Earn (gigs/hustles) → Spend (gear/venues/lifestyle) → Build (venues & crew) → Rise (region by
region) → Fall (Humanity drains, the villain emerges).** Rewards scale every act
(see [05-world-and-regions](05-world-and-regions.md)).

## Signature systems

### 1. Play-as-anyone (Watch Dogs: Legion)
At high heat you **hot-swap between characters** to solve a scene from multiple angles. The flagship
example (Ep 28, *SWAT 3-to-4 Stars*): the raid hits, you start as **Pete**, switch to a **D-Boy** to
clear an exit, then to **OnDeKwa** to slip the cordon — even switching **from SWAT's side** to see
the raid from the law's perspective.

### 2. The DJ Battle — "Jack the Beats" (Naruto-style circle)
Head-to-head duels happen in a **battle circle**. You **read** an opponent's style and **counter**
it (genre rock-paper-scissors + timing). You choose to **befriend** a DJ (collab, unlock, ally) or
**beef** them (sabotage, humiliate, take their spot). Fights can be **themed to songs** — a Kenya
house-set boss runs as a multi-phase fight: *Lion Love → Mashup → Party Don't Stop (remix) → Gone in
AYBB → No Kitty → So Sexy → Chicago* (+ ending cutscene).

### 3. Nightlife
- **After-party hopping** — chain venues across a night map.
- **Intoxication** — *shots, smoke, pop*; bartenders push you, balance buzz vs. control.
- **Social** — work the room; build **clout**; "score" / flirt; risk relationships.
- **The dream sequence** — when faded, the **player's POV shifts and colours change**.

### 4. Streets
- **Sneaking / dealing** — move "Pusha P" quietly; stealth + route planning.
- **Heat / wanted level** — raids, bail, bribes, ditching evidence.
- **Combat** — fists, weapons, crew brawls; **ambulance/triage** when it goes wrong.

### 5. School (Bully-style)
At the Royal Music School: the **hangover-to-class** minigame — wake up wrecked, sprint across
campus, make the lecture; plus exams that gate the **two-year scholarship** meter.

### 6. Sports & traversal minigames
Basketball, soccer, cricket, rugby, **boxing** (a full mini-arc + title belt), **street racing**,
and **skateboarding** — each boosts stats and unlocks side income.

### 7. Help-a-friend businesses
Bootstrap your crew's ventures (merch, a label, a venue) — a light business sim that feeds the city
economy and crew-loyalty meter.

### 8. Quality-of-life
Waiting for a **taxi / Uber**, fast-travel between owned venues, and a phone hub (contacts, gigs,
socials — post photos to "Fakebook" for clout).

## The Humanity meter (the villain dial)
Dark choices (betrayals, the heist path, taking the Komedian power) drain **Humanity**. It changes
dialogue, which crews follow you, and which **ending** you get. It is the mechanical engine of the
**Kid → General Grievous → Z** descent.
