# DJ24 XD — World & Regions

The campaign is a **world tour** across Planet Ongaku. The four playable regions are themed to the
four **suits**, and each suit also defines the **enemy "house"** you fight there. You start small in
the hometown, then travel suit by suit, with **payouts climbing every region**.

## The four suits → four regions

| Suit | Region theme | Enemy house | In-game region |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ❀ **Flowers** | **Africa**-inspired city | Africa-type opposition | Home turf (the come-up) |
| ♥ **Hearts** | **Asia / Europe** | Hearts house | The **Royal Music School** |
| ♦ **Diamonds** | **Middle-Eastern royalty** | Diamonds house | Gold City (the industry) |
| ♠ **Spades** | **America / extreme skyscrapers** | Spades house | **Ongaku Prime** (the capital) |

> **"Give your loved one a flower."** The Flowers motif is romantic as well as regional — the
> Hearts arc literally has a *Give Her a Flower* beat (Ep 38). Flowers = home & love; Spades = the
> dark, vertical capital.

## Ongaku Prime
The Spades capital — endless skyscrapers, the seat of the **King**, **Command HQ**, and the senate
spire. It ties directly into the existing wiki location **Ongaku Prime** and is where the
**Komedian Invasion** and the **Regicide** finale play out.

## Map philosophy
A dense, neon, **Cyberpunk-2077-Night-City**-style world ("hammer the city"), region-gated like GTA:
San Andreas — each region opens only after you clear the previous act's finale boss.
