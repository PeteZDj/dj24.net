# DJ24 XD — Protagonist & Cast

## The Protagonist — "Pete" (player avatar)
DJ24 XD is an **RPG: you create your own character**. "Pete" (PeteZDj) is the default identity, but
the player customises look, name, and a starting **class/stat lean**:

- **Stats:** Mixing, Crowd Read, Charm, Stamina, Stealth, Heat (notoriety), and **Humanity**
  (a hidden meter that drains as the story darkens — at 0, the villain endings unlock).
- **Arc identity:** the avatar is renamed by the world as the story progresses — **Kid → General
  Grievous → Z**.

## NPC archetypes (the living city)
Every venue and street is populated by interactive archetypes:

- **Bouncers** — gate the venues (bribe / connect / sneak / fight).
- **Security** — escalating force inside high-end spots.
- **Promoters** — hand out gigs, passes, and beefs.
- **Chicks / Pro** — social & romance targets; some are scams.
- **Homies / Crew / Squad** — your recruitable network.
- **Bartenders** — try to get you drunk (intoxication system).
- **Dealers** — the buy/consume and resell (Pusha P) economy.
- **Opposition** — rival DJs and crews.
- **Fans** — clout, requests, and crowd energy.

## How the existing DJ24 universe plugs in
DJ24 XD is **canon-compatible** with the wiki. Established characters appear as mentors, bosses, and
faction powers so the game "works with our current characters":

- **DJ24 guardians** (e.g. **Sync**, **General 24**, **Molly**, **MasterBass**) run **Command HQ**
  and try to recruit/stop Pete in the Spades arc. **Sync** is the final guardian boss.
- **The Sick 52** (e.g. **Red Silence**, **Bass Phantom**, **Melody Hex**) are the dangerous
  alliance Pete uses and then double-crosses. They map to the existing
  **[/sick52](../../src/)** roster and **[/missions](../../src/missionsData.js)** bounty board.
- **SubZ** — frequency pirate who becomes Pete's underworld partner (blackouts, heists, the Castle).
- **The Komedians** — the existing **Joker / Komedian Invasion** faction, here given their origin as
  planet-miners.

> Cross-links in the game data use the same slugs as the wiki, so an episode that features a Sick 52
> card can deep-link to that character's existing page.
