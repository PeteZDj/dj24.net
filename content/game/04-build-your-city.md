# DJ24 XD — Build Your City (the Chess System)

You don't just buy safehouses like GTA — you **build an empire of venues**, and the tiers are named
after **chess pieces**. Each tier is bigger, richer, and harder to run than the last. Owning higher
tiers is how passive income (and notoriety) scales across the acts.

## Venue tiers (chess pieces)

| Piece | Venue | What it is | First unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ♟ **Pawn** | **House Party** | Backyard / block party. Your first buildable venue. | Flowers (Ep 17) |
| ♝ **Bishop** | **Mega Private Party / Festival start** | An invite-only mega party; the start of show/festival promotion. | Flowers finale → Hearts |
| ♞ **Knight** | **High-End Nightclub** | A superstar-tier club with celebrity bookings. | Diamonds (Ep 55 unlock) |
| ♛ **Queen** | **Strip Club / Unique Experience** | A one-of-one, ultra-exclusive experience nobody can copy. | Diamonds (Ep 63) |
| ♚ **King** | **Mega Festival** | A "Coachella"-scale, planet-broadcast festival. | Spades (Ep 84) |
| ♜ **Castle (Rook)** | **EDM Underground Rave** | A hidden, lawless rave network — your shadow power base. | Spades (Ep 81) |

> **Why chess?** The pieces double as a power metaphor for the villain arc: you start as a Pawn and
> end by committing **Regicide** — taking the King (and the literal king of Ongaku).

## The 8 cribs (homes / bases)
Separate from venues, you collect up to **8 home properties**, each with a gameplay function:

1. **Crib** — default home; wardrobe & save.
2. **Trap House** — dealing / stash logistics; reduces heat on the streets.
3. **BnB(s)** — rentable income while you travel between regions.
4. **Home Studio** — produce tracks; raises the ceiling on DJ-battle moves.
5. **Penthouse** — Diamonds-tier; unlocks elite contacts.
6. **Beach House** — recovery (Stamina/Humanity soft regen) & social events.
7. **Tour Bus** — mobile base for festival promotion (Bishop/King).
8. **Warehouse** — Castle/rave logistics & heist staging in the Spades.

## Economy notes
- Venues generate **passive payouts** between missions; upgrade them to raise the cap.
- The **map is constructible** — you place and upgrade venues on plots you win or buy, GTA-style but
  with the chess ladder gating what you're allowed to build in each region.
