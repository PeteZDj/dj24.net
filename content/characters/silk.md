## Overview

> *"Men think power is having the loudest speaker or the biggest gun. Power is deciding whose name is on the list, who gets a table, and who never enters the building again."*

Nadia "Silk" Silk rules the glamorous, cutthroat nightlife sector of Planet Ongaku. Climbing from a teenage door-hostess in Rose Hill to the head of the Velvet Static empire, she conquered Hearts—a house historically dominated by old-money elites—through relentless intelligence gathering, contract leverage, and cold operational discipline.

Always dressed in bespoke ivory-and-magenta silk tailoring with glowing fiber-optic seam piping and gold-tipped stiletto nails, Silk operates out of the penthouse lounge of **The Velvet Room**. She fiercely enforces a "No Static Cut" policy in her clubs, knowing that street drugs degrade the prestige and profitability of the sound culture.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Audits VIP reservations, reviews artist booking contracts for the coming festival season, and meets with distributors at The Velvet Room.
- **Home:** A sleek, minimalist penthouse overlooking the Neon District skyline, completely private with no staff permitted after 8 PM.
- **People:** Estranged from her mother in Southside; maintains a close, unspoken alliance with Rosaline Kade ("Mama Kade").
- **Weekly Routine:** Swims 40 laps at 5:00 AM every single morning in an Olympic-sized infinity pool overlooking the city before anyone else is awake.
- **Vice:** Never drinks alcohol or uses stimulants; absolute self-control is her addiction. Collects ultra-expensive custom gold jewelry.
- **Secret:** She anonymously funds a state-of-the-art classical and jazz music conservatory for underprivileged children in her old Southside neighborhood.
