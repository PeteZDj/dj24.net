## Overview

> *"Everyone wants to put a card on my chest. Grand Verse wants Spades. Bishop wants Clubs. Silk wants Hearts. I just want to lay my tracks without looking over my shoulder every time a siren goes off."*

Amara "Freq Kid" Nwosu is the 19-year-old musical prodigy of Southside and the primary audience point-of-view into the street layer of Planet Ongaku. Born into the tenements beneath the Sound Towers, she won three consecutive city-wide cyphers before any label knew her government name.

Armed with an MPC beat-pad tablet that calculates real-time cyan waveform harmonics and a vintage gold dynamic mic, Freq Kid is gifted with natural rhythm-sync sensitivity that rivals formal [[Syncopate]] training.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Rehearsing in a damp basement studio behind an auto-parts warehouse; spending hours cutting drum loops and tracking vocal takes.
- **Home:** A third-floor walkup apartment above a laundromat in Southside, living with her ailing grandmother.
- **People:** Her grandmother Ma Nwosu; her producer K-Oven (whom she suspects is leaking tracks to Clubs); and her neighborhood friends.
- **Weekly Routine:** Sits on the concrete stoop of her building every evening from 11 PM to 1 AM writing lyrics into beat-pads while the street traffic rumbles past.
- **Vice:** Spending her recording advances buying medicine and groceries for neighborhood families instead of investing in personal gear.
- **Secret:** Grand Verse's underboss has already slipped the **10 of Spades card** into her pocket during a private meeting; she has not returned it, nor has she played it.

---

## Signature Gear & Audio Weaponry

- **The Cyan Waveform MPC Tablet:** A heavily customized sampler tablet with an analog vacuum tube preamp. It can tap into local civic sound relays to pulse bass drops capable of disorienting police and rival enforcers.
- **The Golden Dynamic Shure 55:** A vintage gold-plated microphone modified with a short-range sonic compression filter. When she projects her voice, it can shatter glass and disrupt radio signals.
- **Street-Lit LED High-Tops:** Customized athletic kicks with pressure-sensitive piezoelectric soles that recharge her audio battery packs with every step she takes.

---

## Signature Moves (Game Systems)

1. **Open Mic:** Takes over a pirate broadcast or club sound system. Drastically spikes district Reach and street renown without raising law-enforcement Heat.
2. **The Empty Seat:** Her status as the most sought-after recruit causes diplomatic friction between all four Mafia houses, freezing hostile actions between them.
3. **Not Yet (Ultimate):** Refuses the Made offer. By staying an unsigned free agent, she dodges targeted faction hits and operates as a wild-card operative who can access both rebel and mafia spaces.
