## Overview

> *"You think getting Made means you made it? It means you owe everybody. The studio fee, the car note, the cut to the corner captain. Every time my phone buzzes at 3:00 AM, my heart jumps out my chest. Don't take the card, Amara. Run."*

Darius "Half-Step" Pryor is the cautionary reality of the Hip Hop Mafia’s bottom tier. At 19, he is gaunt, skinny, and wiry, with sunken cheekbones and wide, anxious eyes that constantly scan the rooftops and alley mouths of Southside. Dressed in a frayed, oversized olive-drab and black bomber jacket loaded with dangling patch cables, baggy utility pants, and scuffed high-tops, Darius looks like every desperate street-level dealer hustling on the rain-slick asphalt.

He is the nephew of Otis "Deacon" Pryor (5♠), the respected administrator of the Kade Community Trust. When Darius started producing underground beats that caught heat in local cyphers, he dreamed of making tracks for Grand Verse. Instead, to pay off debts from leased studio equipment and a car he couldn't afford, he accepted the 10 of Spades seat last year. 

Now, he is trapped at the very bottom of the ladder: selling contraband sound sample drives in back alleys, running burner errands for 8-Bar, and serving as the direct living warning to Freq Kid of what happens when a prodigy signs with the syndicate.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Sitting on an overturned milk crate behind an all-night diner, swapping encrypted sample flash drives with nervous teenage beatmakers.
- **Home:** A damp basement bachelor flat in Southside with water damage on the ceiling and an analog synthesizer set on plastic milk crates.
- **People:** His uncle Deacon (5♠), who tries to lecture him about responsibility; his mother, whom he hides his bruises from; and Freq Kid, whom he desperately warns away from Commission meetings.
- **Weekly Routine:** Sits in his leased car outside Boyd & Sons Barbershop every Friday night, waiting for 8-Bar's runners to collect the weekly street envelope.
- **Vice:** Energy drinks, cheap cigarettes, and spending whole nights obsessively re-tuning 808 kick-drum transients.
- **Secret:** Kept a duplicate encrypted flash drive containing unreleased vocal acapellas stolen from Two-Track Studios, praying it will be his buyout ticket if the syndicate collapses.

## Equipment & Audio Arsenal

- **Chest-Rigged Mini Beat-Pad:** A battered 8-pad portable sampler slung diagonally across his chest like a dealer's pouch. Loaded with audio scramblers, street beats, and encrypted flash-storage slots for illegal sonic capsules.
- **Foam-Cushioned Retro Earphones:** Inexpensive orange-foam headphones hung around his neck, rigged with a shortwave frequency sniffer that chirps whenever police radio channels close in.
- **Glow-Drive Contraband Capsules:** Cyan-glowing high-density USB capsules loaded with bootleg audio stems, untaxed sound loops, and pirate radio frequencies sold in back alleys.
- **Unfired Compact Pistol:** Kept buried at the bottom of his bomber pocket. He hates touching it and prays every night he will never have to draw it.
