## Overview

> *"Uncle Marcus doesn't ride with guards. He rides with me. Because by the time an ambush spots our taillights, we're already three corners and two districts away."*

June "Cousin June" Cole is the 22-year-old niece and primary driver of Marcus "Grand Verse" Cole. Lean, athletic, and fiercely quick-witted, June embodies the agile street intelligence that keeps the Spades upper crust alive. With gold-amber tipped cornrows pulled into a high ponytail, yellow cyber racing shades, and a signature black-and-gold track jacket with glowing equalizer piping, June is instantly recognizable across Southside street circuits.

Unlike the nervous rookies or heavy bruisers, June moves through the underworld with relaxed confidence. She has driven manual sports cars through police cordons since she was fifteen, navigating Southside's alley networks with pinpoint spatial memory. When Grand Verse needs to deliver sensitive verbal orders to 8-Bar or meet with the Commission, June is the only person behind the wheel.
