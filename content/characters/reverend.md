## Overview

> *"A soft tongue breaketh the bone. When you pull a gun, you admit that your words have lost their authority. Put the piece away, sit at the table, and let us break bread before you spill blood you cannot afford to pay for."*

Ezra "Reverend" Boone is the conscience and diplomatic shield of the Hip Hop Mafia. In his mid-forties, tall, serene, and soft-spoken with silver wire-rimmed spectacles and a tailored clerical-cut coat embroidered with subtle gold soundwave trim, Boone is the only person on Planet Ongaku permitted to walk freely between Spades, Clubs, Hearts, and Diamonds territory without an escort.

He holds the Jack of Spades seat not as an enforcer, but as the Commission’s neutral mediator. When rival corner captains draw weapons over distribution borders or when high-ranking made members are captured, Reverend is called to convene a Sanctuary Table. He carries no weapons, relying instead on his encyclopedic knowledge of syndicate bylaws, family debts, and the binding Commission Covenant.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Supervising the church food pantry and mentoring young teenagers trying to exit corner crews.
- **Home:** A modest rectory apartment attached to the Southside Community Centre.
- **People:** The mothers of Southside soldiers; Grand Verse (whom he counsels in private); and Mama Kade, with whom he shares charity funds.
- **Weekly Routine:** Conducts Wednesday evening youth choir rehearsals and Thursday open community dinners where anyone can eat without fear.
- **Vice:** Hand-brewed herbal teas, leather-bound theological treatises, and classical organ compositions.
- **Secret:** Holds the confessions of thirty-two Made members—including confessions of murders that would shatter the Commission if ever revealed.
