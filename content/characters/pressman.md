## Overview

> *"The big labels think digital DRM keeps a song locked in the clouds. But physical wax? You can't remotely delete a vinyl groove pressed in gold and lacquer. As long as I've got my lathe, the streets hear what they want to hear."*

Ray "Pressman" Duvall is the resident audio anarchist of the House of Spades. At 24, Ray is lean, wiry, and energetic, sporting messy anime spikes tipped in electric teal, a rebellious smirk, and grease smudges from industrial pressing machinery across his cheek. Decked out in a patch-covered denim battle vest, loose distressed hoodie, and mechanical work gloves, Ray looks like an underground cyberpunk record store owner turned pirate manufacturer.

Operating out of an old steam-powered pressing plant hidden in Southside's industrial rail yards, Ray uses restored hydraulic presses and customized laser cutting styluses to engrave bootleg audio onto heavy 180g gold vinyl. When Grand Verse needs to leak a damaging unreleased diss track or distribute untraceable sonic frequencies across the city without leaving digital server breadcrumbs, Pressman stamps ten thousand physical copies overnight.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Calibrating the heated lacquer cutting arm on his vintage Neumann lathe while monitoring police frequency scanners.
- **Home:** A mezzanine bedroom suspended over the noisy pressing plant floor, smelling of warm vinyl pellets and solder.
- **People:** His apprentice crew of street urchins who box and deliver 12-inch singles across the four districts; and Two-Track (6♠), who feeds him master studio tapes.
- **Weekly Routine:** Late-night test listening sessions through 1970s McIntosh tube amplifiers with acoustic earplugs.
- **Vice:** Collecting sealed original pressings of 1990s boom-bap records; refusing to listen to MP3 or streaming audio.
- **Secret:** Possesses a set of stamper plates containing the master session of a legendary lost album whose publication could bring down the entire Diamond House council.
