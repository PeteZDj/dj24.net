## Overview

> *"Marcus commands the soldiers in the streets. But every lyric they recite, every beat that rattles the concrete, and every dollar that enters their accounts belongs to my tape vaults. Cross Marcus, and he puts you in the ground. Cross me, and I erase your voice from history."*

Delores "Ladysoul" Cole is the regal, untouchable sovereign of the Hip Hop Mafia. In her late fifties, with majestic silver-streaked afro updo, piercing dark amber eyes, and luxurious obsidian-and-gold silk kimono-cut coats, Delores commands absolute deference across Planet Ongaku. She is the older sister of Grand Verse and co-architect of the Cole dynasty.

While Grand Verse deals in current muscle and street treaties, Delores controls the historical soul of the culture: the master recordings. In the climate-controlled vaults beneath Ladysoul Records lie the original multi-track session tapes of the foundational hip-hop, gospel, and soul albums that defined the planet before the exile. She collects royalty revenues from all four districts, and her approval is required for any major syndicate financial maneuver.

- **Work on Tuesday:** Auditing tape preservation logs and inspecting 2-inch magnetic audio reels in the deep vault beneath Ladysoul Records.
- **Home:** A sprawling historic brownstone mansion on the Southside ridge with an enclosed rooftop conservatory.
- **People:** Her brother Marcus (Grand Verse); her niece June (9♠); and Mama Kade (Consigliere), her lifelong confidante.
- **Weekly Routine:** Sits on her garden terrace at dusk drinking jasmine tea while listening to unreleased 1970s soul masters through custom electrostatic speakers.
- **Vice:** Collecting rare pre-war vinyl pressings, antique gold fountain pens, and high-grade jasmine pearls.
- **Secret:** Holds the unmastered original recordings of the session that sparked the first Syndicate War — a tape that proves the Diamond House stole their initial fortune from Southside artists.
