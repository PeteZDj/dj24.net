## Overview

> *"When a crew steps into Southside without paying the gate tax, I don't send lawyers. I sit them in my chair, spin it around, and give them eight bars of rhythm to explain themselves. By bar nine, the decision is already made."*

Terrence "8-Bar" Boyd is the muscular, battle-hardened right hand of Grand Verse. In his early forties, with a razor-sharp barbershop fade, immaculate edge-up, sculpted beard, and a scar cutting across his left brow, Boyd radiates disciplined, no-nonsense street authority. He wears a heavy shearling black leather bomber jacket with gold embroidered eight-bar musical notation across the chest, thick Cuban gold links, and a heavy chrome sonic sidearm holstered at his shoulder.

While Grand Verse presides over the high-level politics and boardroom publishing treaties at Vantaggio's, 8-Bar controls the pavement. His chain of barbershops across Southside acts as the nerve center for street-level intelligence: every bookie, corner lookout, and club bouncer answers to his captains.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Personally cutting hair at the Southside flagship chair from 8 AM to noon, holding private conversations while clipping edges.
- **Home:** A heavily secured brick townhouse in the Old Quarter with private subterranean garage access.
- **People:** His two sons who apprentice at the shops; his corner commanders; and Grand Verse, whom he has backed for over twenty years.
- **Weekly Routine:** Reviews the weekly street counts every Thursday night in the backroom of the barbershop over bourbon and jazz.
- **Vice:** Handcrafted bespoke straight razors, vintage boxing film reels, and expensive shearling leather coats.
- **Secret:** Has maintained back-channel communications with Hector "Ox" Delgado (K♣) to ensure that if the four Aces ever go to war, the soldiers won't slaughter each other needlessly.
