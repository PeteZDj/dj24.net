## Overview

> *"The deck came first. They counted themselves in cards long before the Council broke the world. The Sick 52 didn't invent the deck — they stole it. And I don't forgive thieves."*

Marcus Cole is the supreme authority of Planet Ongaku's criminal underworld. A legendary lyricist who transitioned from street-corner cyphers to boardroom empires, he created Verse Holdings to give the culture a landlord who looked like it—only to learn that landlords need soldiers.

Soft-spoken, immaculate, and patient, Grand Verse governs with psychological weight rather than open violence. Clad in an opulent black cashmere trench coat lined with glowing gold equalizer embroidery, he carries a gold-capped walking cane that conceals an ultrasonic frequency blade.

## The Civilian Column

Every major character on Planet Ongaku lives an ordinary life alongside their mythic role:

- **Work on Tuesday:** Sits in his penthouse corner office at Verse Holdings signing distribution rights, auditing royalty distributions, and meeting with corporate liaisons.
- **Home:** A sprawling, private estate in The Heights, living alone since his wife passed fifteen years ago.
- **People:** His sister Delores ("Ladysoul", Q♠), his underboss Tunde ("8-Bar", K♠), and his cousin June (9♠).
- **Weekly Routine:** **Sunday 7:00 AM golf** with the same three retired civic officials, unbroken for 22 years. Eats breakfast alone at Vantaggio's at 11:30 AM before the Commission arrives.
- **Vice:** Rare vintage vinyl pressings purchased at absurd black-market auctions.
- **Secret:** Despite being celebrated as Ongaku's greatest living lyricist, **he has never learned to read sheet music**; he hears everything by instinct.

## Signature Gear & Audio Weaponry

- **The Golden Cane of Solitude:** A custom gold-capped ebony walking cane with an integrated ultra-high frequency acoustic blade that slices matter by vibrating at resonance.
- **Acoustic Dampening Signets:** Solid gold rings on both hands tuned to neutralize incoming kinetic shockwaves from rival sound attacks.
- **The Unfired Nickel .45:** Kept in a velvet-lined desk drawer. Unfired in twenty years because his word has never failed to resolve a crisis.
- **The '68 Kestrel Continental:** A restored vintage luxury cruiser fitted with sound-dampened double-glazed armor glass and a discrete sub-bass dispersal exhaust.
