## Overview

> *"NexaGen builds high-speed transport for the military and orbital probes for the billionaires. I steal their telemetry, reverse-engineer their sub-light drives, and put them in a street machine. You can't catch what you can't hear coming."*

Dae-Sun "Chrome" Pak is the charismatic, hyper-talented mechanical genius behind the illegal street-racing scene of Planet Ongaku. Born in the gritty shipping docks of the Harbour District, he could rebuild complex Sakata hover-engines blindfolded before he turned sixteen.

Recruited into Diamonds after successfully hijacking an experimental NexaGen prototype transport, Chrome runs the **Redline Garage**: a massive subterranean chop shop where corporate tech is repurposed into modified street racers and armored getaway vehicles. With spiky bleached-blonde hair, glowing soundwave neck tattoos, and a handheld holographic diagnostic tuner, Chrome lives for high-octane adrenaline.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Tuning turbocharged induction sound-cannons on street-racing coupes, bribing container shipping inspectors, and flashing modified ECU chips.
- **Home:** A messy industrial loft situated directly above the main bay of Redline Garage, filled with engine blocks, spare tires, and blueprints.
- **People:** His younger sister Hana (an engineering student at Ongaku University whose tuition he pays); his crew of tuner mechanics.
- **Weekly Routine:** Midnight test-runs across the elevated industrial expressways of the Harbour District, timing quarter-mile sprints against radar detectors.
- **Vice:** Eating cheap instant spicy ramen directly out of the bowl while sitting on engine blocks; refuses to eat at expensive restaurants.
- **Secret:** He has an ongoing covert romance with **Lt. Sana "Rook" Delacroix**, an elite interceptor pilot in Aero Command who illegally races with his crew on her weekends off.

## Signature Gear & Audio Weaponry

- **Holographic Diagnostic Tuner:** A wrist-and-handheld cyber-deck that projects orange 3D holographic wireframes of vehicle schematics, allowing him to hack into automotive networks, traffic grids, and drone sensors mid-chase.
- **The Midnight-Purple Redline Beast:** A heavily customized, widebody cyber-muscle car equipped with a sub-bass acoustic displacement engine and cyan LED underglow, capable of generating sonic shockwaves that flip pursuing police cruisers.
- **Resonance-Wired Racing Jacket:** A reinforced black leather racing jacket woven with illuminated cyan fiber-optic cables that monitor his vital signs and adrenaline during 200 MPH pursuits.

## Signature Moves (Game Systems)

1. **Redline Extraction:** When a mission goes wrong, calling in Chrome guarantees an automatic successful extraction on any Wheels check, outrunning SWAT and ODF cordons.
2. **Chop Shop Liquidation:** Converts stolen NexaGen vehicle tech into clean Diamonds money, yielding massive revenue bonuses between story chapters.
3. **Built This Month (Ultimate):** Deploys a completely unique, one-off prototype vehicle outfitted with experimental acoustic shielding, enabling your squad to breach heavily guarded fortresses or bypass military radar undetected.
