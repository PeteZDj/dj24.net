## Overview

> *"You make noise when you're afraid. When I step in the room, even the streetlamps stop humming. If you hear me breathe, it means you're still alive."*

Kenneth Sayles is a 6-foot-6 physical juggernaut and the most feared enforcer in Ongaku Prime. A former heavyweight boxer rescued from a street gang by Grand Verse, he became the instrument of the Commission's violent will.

Unlike most warriors on Ongaku who fight with loud sonic blasts, Kenneth wields **Dead-Air Vacuum Manipulation**: an ability that swallows sound waves, creating terrifying zones of total silence where kinetic strikes land with devastating, unannounced force. Deeply devout and scarred by the atrocities he has committed, Kenneth wears a heavy silver crucifix and refuses to participate in the drug trade.

## The Civilian Column

- **Work on Tuesday:** Wakes at 4:30 AM to unlock his boxing gym in Southside. Spends all morning coaching neighborhood teenagers and working the heavy bags alone.
- **Home:** A modest two-bedroom apartment situated directly above the boxing gym, where he lives with his wife and two young daughters.
- **People:** His wife Miriam (who believes he only does commercial building security); his daughters; and Father Matthew at St. Jude's.
- **Weekly Routine:** Attends Sunday morning service at Southside Chapel, sitting in the front pew in a clean dark suit. Never misses a sermon.
- **Vice:** None. He does not drink, smoke, gamble, or take cuts from narcotics. His absolute discipline is what makes him terrifying.
- **Secret:** He has secretly established an anonymous bank account to fund an exit from Ongaku for his family, waiting for the day he can break his oath to the deck.

## Signature Gear & Audio Weaponry

- **Dead-Air Carbon Knuckle Gloves:** Reinforced combat gauntlets fitted with phase-cancellation micro-emitters. Every punch deploys a localized sonic vacuum, muffling impacts and breaking bones silently.
- **Sound-Absorbing Tactical Vest:** Heavy matte-black body armor lined with acoustic aerogel that dampens incoming audio attacks and concussive blasts.
- **The Silver Crucifix:** A heavy sterling silver cross worn around his neck, his anchor to morality in an otherwise merciless profession.
- **Matte-Black Wolfe Coupé:** A heavy domestic muscle car whose engine and exhaust are completely suppressed with sound-deadening alloys, allowing him to arrive and depart like a ghost.

## Signature Moves (Game Systems)

1. **Dead Air:** Emits a 15-meter sphere of total silence. Disables enemy sound-based special attacks, sonic blades, and radio communication for the duration of the combat round.
2. **Refuses the Cut:** Automatically rejects orders involving narcotics or civilian intimidation. Prevents Clubs and Diamonds from using him for illegal product protection.
3. **The Breaking Point (Ultimate):** The most dangerous flip risk in the game. If Commission pressure forces him to compromise his moral code, his Loyalty drops to 0 and he flips—becoming an unstoppable rogue hunter targeting the Mafia's leadership.
