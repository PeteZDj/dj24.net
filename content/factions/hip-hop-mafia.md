# Hip Hop Mafia — The Made Deck

The **Made Deck** came first. An old street ranking — fifty-two Made members, plus two Jokers — that
predates the exile. When the [[Sick 52]] dealt themselves as a living deck in mockery of the streets,
**[[Grand Verse]]** took it as plagiarism. That grudge still sits at the Commission table.

They are not mutants. They do not bend frequency. They run corners, clubs, kitchens, and books —
with **guns, cars, fronts**, and a Loyalty stat that can flip a whole campaign.

> Motto: *"The beat is rented. The block is owned."*

---

## Goals

- **Control the culture economy** — masters, venues, publishing, the door
- **Keep Static Cut profitable** without letting it burn the houses that want out
- **Survive the Tower story** — a journalist is circling the Commission
- **Settle the empty seat** — and decide what to do about [[Freq Kid]]

## Role in the Story

Where the Sick 52 are a war, the Mafia is a **crime procedural**. You take cards by building cases,
flipping low-Loyalty seats, and climbing toward A♠ in an interview room — not a boss fight.

The two ladders cross: a case on Clubs product leads to the Pressing Plant; Blackout wants out;
Chrome's garage touches NexaGen Motion.
